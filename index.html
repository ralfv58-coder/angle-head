Angle Head - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Angle Head ("the app") is made by Ralf's Automotive.

The app collects no personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not use the internet.

The few settings you choose (which way your head swings and which way each dial counts) are stored only on your own phone, inside the app. They are never sent anywhere, and removing the app removes them.

The app does not ask for any permissions.
